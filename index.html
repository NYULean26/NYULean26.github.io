Course overview

Mathematics,
made executable.

This course introduces the principles and practice of computer-assisted proof formalization using the Lean 4 proof assistant. We will encode definitions, statements, and proofs with machine-checked guarantees of correctness.

Along the way, we will study dependent type theory, the architecture of Mathlib, Lean metaprogramming, and recent approaches to proof search and automatic translation from informal mathematics.

why_lean.md×
$

A proof assistant turns rigor from an aspiration into a compile-time guarantee.

By the end of the course, you will be able to…

  1. 01

    Read, write, and debug Lean 4, from definitions and theorem statements to complete proofs.

  2. 02

    Reason with Lean’s foundations, including propositions-as-types, dependent types, universes, and inductive types.

  3. 03

    Translate informal mathematics into machine-checkable statements and critically assess formalizations by others.

  4. 04

    Navigate and reuse Mathlib, recognizing abstraction patterns and adapting library results to new problems.

  5. 05

    Extend and evaluate the prover through simple metaprogramming, tactics, and AI-assisted theorem proving.

Course policies

Clear expectations,
from day one.

Attendance, quizzes & late work

Lecture attendance is encouraged but not required. Quizzes are delivered in person.

A make-up session for missed quizzes will be held in week 10. Notify the instructor in writing by the end of week 9 if you wish to take a missed quiz. Late final projects are not accepted.

Use of generative AI

Weeks 1–8: Generative AI may not be used to prepare submitted homework or quiz materials. Using AI as a homework tutor is discouraged in favor of peer discussion, but is not prohibited.

From week 9: Generative AI is allowed and encouraged for the final project. Its use must be disclosed in detail. The amount of AI assistance does not determine the grade, but every student must be able to explain and work proficiently with every part of the project.
